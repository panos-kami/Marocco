05 · MOVE

Getting around.

Casablanca is large. Walking works within areas, but not for everything.
🚕
Petit taxi
Good for shorter city trips. Confirm the meter or price situation before setting off and keep small cash available.
🚋
Tramway
Useful for several parts of the city and often easier than dealing with traffic.
🚌
Busway
Casablanca also has dedicated Busway lines connected with the wider public transport network.
🚆
Casa Voyageurs
Main rail station and useful reference point if travelling onward or arriving by train.
🚶
Walking
Best once we're already inside the area we want to explore: Habous, Medina, centre, Corniche.
06 · KNOW BEFORE

Small things. Big difference.

MONEY
Keep cash.
Cards are useful, but small purchases, taxis and local shops can still be easier with Moroccan dirhams.
DATA
Have internet.
Maps, translations and transport are dramatically easier with mobile data. SIM or eSIM before becoming desperate.
TAXIS
Agree first.
Check meter / fare expectations before leaving. Avoid discovering the price only at the destination.
SHOPPING
Prices are flexible.
In markets, bargaining can be part of the experience. Stay friendly and walk away if the price feels wrong.
WATER
Easy option: bottled.
Especially when travelling and competing, keep hydration simple and predictable.
OCEAN
Atlantic means Atlantic.
Waves, currents and conditions matter. For surf / water sports, use a proper operator and check the conditions.
KEEP IN MIND
Tourist common sense.
Don't follow random “guides” who suddenly decide to help.
Check taxi expectations before the ride.
Don't flash cash or valuables unnecessarily.
Busy markets: phone and bag awareness.
If something feels strangely complicated, leave.
