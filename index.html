MOROCCO · OCTOBER
14—21
ONE TRIP · ONE CITY · THREE OF US

CASA BLANCA

We came here because apparently fencing had to happen in Morocco.

But I have a feeling the parts we’ll remember most won’t happen on the piste.
THE PARTS IN BETWEEN
01
FIRST · THE CITY

A city we didn’t come for.

Which somehow makes exploring it better. No huge checklist. No pressure to see everything. Just a few places that actually feel worth remembering.
THE ONE WE HAVE TO SEE
Hassan II Mosque
Right beside the Atlantic, huge, dramatic and probably the closest Casablanca gets to a genuine “wow” moment.
SLOW MORNING
Habous
Arches, little shops, Moroccan details and the kind of place where we can wander with absolutely no plan.
GET A LITTLE LOST
Old Medina
Narrow streets, local shops and a very good chance somebody buys something completely unnecessary.
02
THEN · THE ATLANTIC

We have an ocean.

Which feels like a pretty good reason not to spend every free hour indoors.
AÏN DIAB · CORNICHE · DAR BOUAZZA
Sunset, beach, a long walk and nowhere urgent to be.
This is probably the easiest part of Casablanca to imagine actually doing after fencing. Ocean, something to drink, something to eat, and staying until the sky looks good enough that nobody wants to leave yet.
SURFING? ABSOLUTELY NOT. WE CAN WATCH.
03
VERY IMPORTANT · FOOD

Things worth trying together.

This part of the trip has a significantly higher probability of success than the surfing section.
Tagine
The obvious Moroccan classic. Slow cooked, warm, heavily spiced and definitely happening.
Pastilla
Sweet and savoury together. Sounds suspicious. Apparently works.
Msemen
Flaky Moroccan flatbread. Breakfast, snack, or whatever we call it when we eat it twice.
Fresh Fish
We are next to the Atlantic. Ignoring this would be unnecessarily stupid.
Mint Tea
Probably sweeter than expected. Still mandatory at least once.
Moroccan Sweets
Honey, almonds, sesame, pastry and temporary suspension of all athletic responsibility.
04
BEST CASE SCENARIO

One day that goes perfectly.

Not a strict itinerary. Just the version of a free day that would actually feel good.
MORNING
Coffee + Habous
Start slowly, wander around, look at little shops, buy something we definitely didn’t plan to buy.
LUNCH
Something Moroccan
No complicated plan. Just find somewhere that smells good and make a decision.
AFTERNOON
Hassan II Mosque
The one major thing we really should not leave Casablanca without seeing.
SUNSET
Corniche
Ocean, no rush, maybe food again, probably a lot of photos.
NIGHT
Dinner + rooftop
Somewhere nice, slightly dressed up, and for a few hours completely forgetting why we came to Morocco.
05
AFTER FENCING

Depends how alive we are.

Probably the most honest part of the whole guide.
10%
Food, shower, bed. Casablanca will survive without us.
40%
Ocean, something to eat, one nice walk. More than enough.
70%
Dinner somewhere nice, rooftop afterwards, stay out longer than planned.
100%
Completely suspicious. Somebody check whether we actually fenced.
THE PART I ACTUALLY CARE ABOUT
Maybe fencing is the reason we’re going to Casablanca.
But I don’t think that will be the part we remember most.

I think it’ll be the random food, the ocean after a long day, getting somewhere later than planned, being tired together, laughing at something stupid, and all the little parts in between that nobody planned.
Casablanca, with you.
